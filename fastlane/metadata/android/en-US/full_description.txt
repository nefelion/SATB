<p>Write four-part (SATB) harmony and check it against common-practice voice-leading rules.</p>

<ul>
<li>Write four voices by tapping notes onto the staff, or play them on a USB or Bluetooth MIDI keyboard.</li>
<li>Harmony check against 25 voice-leading rules (parallel fifths and octaves, leading tone, doubling, spacing …), each explained with an example.</li>
<li>Chord symbols, Roman numerals and named non-chord tones.</li>
<li>Playback with any SoundFont (.sf2) you pick, and export to MusicXML.</li>
</ul>
